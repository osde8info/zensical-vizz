### Enterprise-class. Cloud-proven. Free-of-charge.

This week, Citrix announced a new enterprise-class version of [Citrix XenServer™](http://www.citrix.com/English/ps2/products/feature.asp?contentID=1686939 "citrix")... at no cost. The announcement marks two firsts: the new XenServer is the first (and only) free virtualization offering to include live migration of virtual machines, and it is the first to provide free, centralized multi-server management—both widely considered _killer features_.
