Announcing Amazon SQS WSDL Version 2009-02-01 and Amazon SQS in Europe

[Amazon SQS](http://aws.amazon.com/sqs/) now includes shared queue access which, among other things, will give you the ability to grant access to your queue to anonymous users and enable you to integrate non-AWS applications with AWS applications.
