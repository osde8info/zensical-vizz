[VirtualBox](http://virtualbox.org/ "virtualbox") product has been quickly established as one of the leading developer desktop virtualization platforms.

[Now](http://www.sun.com/aboutsun/pr/2008-02/sunflash.20080212.1.xml "sun"), as part of the Sun xVM portfolio, [VirtualBox](http://virtualbox.org/ "virtualbox") will have the support of Sun's global development community, field resources and partners to make [VirtualBox](http://virtualbox.org/ "virtualbox") even more compelling to developers and end users, driving greater adoption across a broad set of communities.

VirtualBox enables desktop or laptop PCs running the Windows, Linux, Mac or Solaris operating systems to run multiple, different operating systems side-by-side, switching between them with just a click of the mouse.
