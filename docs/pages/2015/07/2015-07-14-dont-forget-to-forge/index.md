dont forget to forget to visit your free openshift vms every 90 days with a web browser otherwise they will be deleted to save money and resources alternatively i guess if you switch from the free cloud to the paid for cloud they wont be deleted either

_Some OpenShift Online applications haven't been accessed recently.  To keep our service optimized for all of our active users, we remove applications from accounts which have been inactive (all apps either stopped or idle) more than 90 days.  In two weeks, your applications will be flagged for removal from OpenShift Online.  This includes the git repository, logs, and any persisted data._
