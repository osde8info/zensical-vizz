If you dont like the standard [AWS Console](http://aws.amazon.com/console/ "aws console") you can pay extra to use the [TurnKey Linux Hub](https://hub.turnkeylinux.org/start/ "turnkey linux hub") (a custom aws console) that lets you start, reboot and stop and [TurnKey Linux](http://www.turnkeylinux.org/ "turnkey linux") VMs and bill the credit card on your AWS account directly. According to the [AWS Calculator](http://calculator.s3.amazonaws.com/calc5.html "aws calculator") running 4 large AMI instances for 24hrs/day will cost you ~ $1113/month.
