The [VI-Toolkit.com](http://www.vi-toolkit.com/wiki/index.php/Main_Page "vi-toolkit") website has been created by [Wil van Antwerpen](http://communities.vmware.com/people/wila "wila") in order to be able to store knowledge about Virtual Infrastructure and related VMware products.

_At start, the site this will contain lots of links to information on the internet and have general information about how-to solve particular problems, but over time the intention is to move the focus slightly over to managing your Virtual Infrastructure using the toolkits available from VMware._
