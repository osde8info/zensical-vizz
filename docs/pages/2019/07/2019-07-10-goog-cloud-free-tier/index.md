goog cloud FREE tier pricing

- https://cloud.google.com/free/docs/gcp-free-tier#always-free-usage-limits

goog cloud FREE tier includes

- _1 non-preemptible f1-micro VM instance per month in one of the specified regions_
- _30 GB-months HDD_
- _5 GB-month snapshot storage in the specified regions_
- _1 GB network egress from North America to all region destinations per month_

_GPUs and TPUs are not included in the Always Free offer. You will always be charged for GPUs and TPUS that you add to instances._
