There's another Software Appliance vendor - [JumpBox](http://www.jumpbox.com/jumpbox-applications "jumpbox sugarcrm")

They have a [Sugar CRM VM](http://www.jumpbox.com/jumpbox-applications "jumpbox sugarcrm") that runs on Parallels, VMware or Xen ! and you can buy support if you want !

There are great tutorials @ [http://www.jumpbox.com/docs/](http://www.jumpbox.com/docs/ "jumpbox") and they have other software appliances such as [Joomla](http://www.jumpbox.com/jumpbox-for-the-joomla-content-management-system "jumpbox joomla") available too
