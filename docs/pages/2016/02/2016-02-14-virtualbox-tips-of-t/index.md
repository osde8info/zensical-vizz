virtualbox tips of the week check bios vt and dont overcommit ram

1. Check your BIOS and confirm VT and VT-x are enabled and then you will be able to build and run 64bit vms in your 64bit os
2. Dont try and run 2 x 1.5G vms if you only have 4G of RAM unless you want to spend 4 hours shutting down - reduce vms to to 1G and restart them
