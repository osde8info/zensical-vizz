AWS Cloud free tier AWS CodeCommit - now you have a third alt to @github and @gitlab

_AWS CodeCommit is FREE for up to 5 users_

- https://aws.amazon.com/free/?all-free-tier.sort-by=item.additionalFields.SortRank&all-free-tier.sort-order=asc&awsf.Free%20Tier%20Types=categories%23alwaysfree
- https://aws.amazon.com/getting-started/projects/migrate-git-repository/?c\_15
- https://aws.amazon.com/codecommit/?ft=n&trk=ft\_card&trk=ft\_card

_The total cost of hosting your project with AWS CodeCommit will vary depending on your usage. Anyone with an AWS account can get started with AWS CodeCommit for free. Your account gets 5 active users per month for free (within limits), after which you pay $1 per additional active user per month. There are no upfront fees or commitments._

_This AWS Free Tier offer for AWS CodeCommit is available to both new and existing AWS customers indefinitely, and does not expire at the end of the standard 12 month Free Tier term._
