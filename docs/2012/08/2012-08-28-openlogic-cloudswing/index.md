OpenLogic CloudSwing is _an open platform-as-a-service (PaaS) solution that enables developers and IT staff to easily deploy and operate applications in the cloud. Designed for the enterprise, CloudSwing acts as your central dashboard for applications across all of the public and private clouds you use._
