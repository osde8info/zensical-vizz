According to [http://www.ibm.com/developerworks/linux/library/l-hadoop/](http://www.ibm.com/developerworks/linux/library/l-hadoop/ "ibm") the _New York Times used Hadoop and Amazon EC2 to convert 4TB of TIFF images - including 405K large TIFF images, 3.3M SGML articles, and 405K XML files - into 800K Web-friendly PNG images in 36 hours._
