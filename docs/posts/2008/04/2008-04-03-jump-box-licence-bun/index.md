[Jump Box Open](http://www.jumpbox.com/jumpbox-open "jumpbox") is a subscription that grants you one license for every JumpBox in the [Open Source Collection](http://www.jumpbox.com/jumpbox-applications "jumpbox") for a period of one year. From Content Management Systems (CMS) to Wikis, CRM to Ticket Tracking, there are already JumpBoxes available for many of the most popular Open Source applications.

There are **20** JumpBoxes currently available, with **50** additional expected by the end of 2008.
