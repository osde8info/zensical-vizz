The [updated version of rBuilder](http://www.rpath.com/corp/2008-press-release-archive/rpath-announces-upgrades-to-rbuilder-and-rpath-appliance-pla.html "rpath rbuilder"), rPath’s flagship product, provides a new Appliance Model which allows software companies to centrally define configurations for the development, testing and release stages of their appliance.  This makes it easier for multiple groups to coordinate on the development and launch of a new appliance.  It also ensures that consistent processes are used across multiple appliance releases.
