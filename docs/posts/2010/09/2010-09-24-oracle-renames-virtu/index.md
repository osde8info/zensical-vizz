last month the www.virtualbox.com and www.virtualbox.org websites seemed to be dead and you could only reach virtualbox via [http://www.oracle.com/virtualization/oraclevm](http://www.oracle.com/us/technologies/virtualization/oraclevm/061976.html) but it seems that this month [http://www.virtualbox.org](http://www.virtualbox.org/) has reappeared and you can download virtualbox from [http://dlc.sun.com/virtualbox/vboxdownload.html](http://dlc.sun.com/virtualbox/vboxdownload.html)!
