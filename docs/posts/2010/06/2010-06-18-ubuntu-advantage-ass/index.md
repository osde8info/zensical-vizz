Canonical Ubuntu have created [Ubuntu Advantage](http://www.canonical.com/enterprise-services/ubuntu-advantage/assurance "ubuntu") and [Ubuntu Assurance](http://www.canonical.com/enterprise-services/ubuntu-advantage/assurance "ubuntu") Programmes

_[Ubuntu Assurance](http://www.canonical.com/enterprise-services/ubuntu-advantage/assurance "ubuntu") covers your business for claims of intellectual property (IP) infringements arising from your use of Ubuntu. This offer is designed to safeguard your business and make deploying Ubuntu even easier._

There are separate programmes for server and desktop

- [ubuntu-advantage/server](//www.canonical.com/enterprise-services/ubuntu-advantage/server "ubuntu")
- [ubuntu-advantage/desktop](http://www.canonical.com/enterprise-services/ubuntu-advantage/desktop "ubuntu")
