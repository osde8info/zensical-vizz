The Xen4CentOS6 project _is a collaborative effort between the Xen Project, the Citrix Xen development teams, the CentOS Project team, GoDaddy Cloud operations group and RackSpace Hosting to package, deliver and maintain a stable Xen hypervisor and its related tooling for CentOS-6, enabling CentOS-6/x86\_64 to be used as a dom0, base platform to host Xen in paravirt and fullvirt deployment roles._
