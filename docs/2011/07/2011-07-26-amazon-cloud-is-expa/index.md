According to [itpro](http://www.itpro.co.uk/635152/amazon-bets-1bn-on-cloud "itpro") the amazon cloud is expanding !

Customers now include _NASA, the US Department of State, Siemens, Pfizer and Nasdaq._

_At the end of the second quarter, Amazon S3 held more than 449 billion objects, up 71 percent from the end of last year, according to the official AWS blog._

And in January _3,674 of the top 500,000 websites were using Amazon EC2_.
