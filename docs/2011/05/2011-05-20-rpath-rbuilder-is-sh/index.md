rPath rBuilder online is [shutting down](http://www.rpath.com/corp/wind-down "rpath") !

_After five years of offering a free community service, rPath has decided to wind down the rBuilder Online hosted service._

however

_they will continue to support the open-source world by offering free rPath software to active open-source communities_ on request

rpath will now be focused on [rpath X6](http://www.rpath.com/corp/rpath-x6 "rpath") ! so you might need to switch to [bitnami](http://bitnami.org/ "bitnami"), [elastic server](https://elasticserver.com/ "elastic server") or [suse studio](http://susestudio.com/ "suse")
