HP, IBM and KVM have created the [Open Virtualisation Alliance](http://www.openvirtualizationalliance.org/ "ova") which will _foster the adoption of KVM as an enterprise-ready open virtualization solution_.

Other members include Intel, Red Hat and Novell Suse.
