[The Cloud Market](http://thecloudmarket.com/ "thecloudmarket") provides a directory of Amazon EC2 images. They _scan Amazon EC2 and pull Image information, then they scan common locations of image information for extra details. They also gather information on the 'owner' or creator of the images and group images by their creators._
