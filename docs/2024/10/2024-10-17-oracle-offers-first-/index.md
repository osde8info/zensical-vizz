Oracle _has announced the first zettascale cloud computing clusters accelerated by the NVIDIA Blackwell platform. [Oracle Cloud Infrastructure](https://www.oracle.com/uk/cloud/) (OCI) are now taking orders for the largest AI supercomputer in the cloud—available with up to 131,072 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs._
