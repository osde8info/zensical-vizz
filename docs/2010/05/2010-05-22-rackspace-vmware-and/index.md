According to [V3](http://www.v3.co.uk/v3/news/2263286/rackspace-brings-cloud-services "v3") the hosting firm [Rackspace](http://blog.rackspace.co.uk/?p=257 "rackspace") is preparing to bring its cloud services into the UK with a Xen-based offering for the SMB market, and hybrid public-private services based on VMware for larger enterprise customers.

_Due in the fourth quarter of 2010, the services will be similar to those already available in the US, but will be delivered from Rackspace datacentres in the UK._

_The company expects to be one of the first major players to site public cloud infrastructure in the UK with this move._

_Rackspace is offering enterprise customers the ability to build a private cloud based on VMware's vCloud platform, and expand it on demand using resources from the public cloud if necessary._
