[OVH Virtual Rack](http://www.ovh.co.uk/items/virtual_rack.xml) _brings together several virtual servers (no matter how many or there physical location in our datacentre) and connect to a virtual switch within the same private network. Your servers can communicate privately and securely between them (in a dedicated VLAN)._
