quay.io is moving to cdn.quay.io

- https://status.quay.io/incidents/s7gfwgmc4dyz

_on Saturday, 25 April 2020 at 4 PM ET quay.io will be rotating the domain name returned for CloudFront based blob requests from its current domain to 'cdn.quay.io'. If you currently whitelist the quay.io CloudFront domain name, please make sure to \*add\* 'cdn.quay.io' to your whitelist_
